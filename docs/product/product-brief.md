# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Spanish-speaking real estate investors, initially in Puerto Rico and later in
Latino communities across the United States. The audience ranges from people
evaluating their first property to active investors managing several deals.
They need to find reliable comparables, understand returns, organize a deal
pipeline, plan financing, and learn from a trusted local network without
stitching together outdated portals, realtor calls, and spreadsheets.

## Product Purpose

Give Latino investors the market visibility and practical tools normally held
by industry insiders. The platform combines public records, MLS information,
and proprietary cash-sale data from a verified realtor network, then turns that
data into clear decisions through comparables, ROI analysis, credit planning,
project estimates, deal tracking, community, and an action-oriented AI
copilot. Success means an investor can determine whether a deal is worth
pursuing with greater speed, confidence, and context.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, candid, and propulsive. The voice is knowledgeable without sounding
institutional, culturally fluent without performing identity, and direct
without becoming aggressive. It should feel like a trusted investor from the
community who has access to the real numbers and explains them clearly.

## Anti-references

- Generic luxury-real-estate branding built from navy, gold, rooflines, keys,
  palm trees, and aspirational stock towers.
- Fintech or crypto aesthetics that make property investing feel abstract,
  speculative, or exclusionary.
- Influencer merchandise branding that depends entirely on Christopher's name
  and cannot become an enduring product company.
- Government-portal stiffness, stale classified-site design, or anything that
  repeats the opacity and friction of current Puerto Rico property sources.
- Dense power-user software that assumes MLS expertise and overwhelms newer
  investors.
- Generic green SaaS branding that could belong to any analytics dashboard.

## Design Principles

1. Show the missing half: make exclusive cash-sale data, source provenance, and
   information freshness tangible.
2. Turn numbers into a next move: every insight should help the investor decide
   what to do.
3. Earn trust in public: expose sources, verification, assumptions, and ranges
   instead of presenting a black box.
4. Sound like the market: use clear Puerto Rican Spanish and familiar industry
   language without jargon theater.
5. Start here, travel well: feel unmistakably born in Puerto Rico while
   remaining credible for Latino investors across the United States.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for core marketing and product journeys. Do not rely on
color alone for source, risk, or deal-quality states. Support keyboard and
screen-reader navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable financial
tables, and plain-language explanations for first-time investors. Preserve
Spanish-first clarity while allowing established English real-estate terms
when they are the language the community actually uses.
