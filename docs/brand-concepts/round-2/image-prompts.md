# Round 2 Brand Concept Prompts

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool in logo-brand mode. Each
concept was generated separately so it could establish a genuinely distinct
identity system.

## Jugada

Create a world-class brand identity concept board for a Spanish-first real
estate investment intelligence platform named JUGADA, born in Puerto Rico and
built for Latino investors. The platform combines verified comparables,
proprietary cash-sale data, ROI calculations, deal tracking, credit planning,
community intelligence, and an action-oriented AI copilot. The strategic idea
is the informed move, never the lucky guess.

Make one cohesive landscape 16:9 identity presentation with a dominant JUGADA
wordmark, a distinctive directional J symbol formed by one deliberate turn,
an app icon, a mobile product screen, a campaign poster, a data-verification
label, a numbered decision path, and a compact ROI result. Use a confident
asymmetric grid with oversized type and useful negative space.

Exact visible Spanish copy, spelled correctly: “JUGADA”; “LA PRÓXIMA JUGADA
EMPIEZA CON DATA.”; “VE EL DEAL COMPLETO.”; “PREPARA TU JUGADA”; “CASH ·
VERIFICADO”; “14.2% ROI”; “3 FUENTES”.

Art direction: strategic, decisive, culturally fluent, energetic, premium
without luxury clichés. Saturated vermilion, powder blue, warm cream, and dark
aubergine. Custom condensed display grotesk with sharp directional details,
paired with a practical humanist sans for data. The graphic language should
show sequences, choices, and momentum.

Do not use sports imagery, balls, stadiums, betting slips, casino motifs,
chess pieces, playing cards, houses, roofs, keys, doors, skylines, palm trees,
Puerto Rico flags, navy and gold, gradients, glassmorphism, crypto styling,
generic green SaaS, fake words, watermarks, or tiny filler text. It must feel
like a real Pentagram or Koto identity case-study board, not a template.

## Criterio

Create a sophisticated, highly distinctive brand identity concept board for a
Spanish-first real estate investment intelligence platform named CRITERIO,
born in Puerto Rico and designed for Latino investors. The platform combines
multiple property sources, proprietary cash-sale comparables, visible data
provenance, ROI analysis, a deal pipeline, education, and community. The
strategic idea is informed judgment built from evidence.

Make one cohesive landscape 16:9 identity presentation with a dominant CRITERIO
wordmark, a recognizable C symbol assembled from evidence marks that resolve
into one clear form, a source stamp, confidence-band graphics, a mobile
comparable screen, a bold campaign poster, a report cover, and a high-confidence
valuation module. Use a rigorous but surprising asymmetric composition.

Exact visible Spanish copy, spelled correctly: “CRITERIO”; “NO ES INTUICIÓN. ES
CRITERIO.”; “DATA PARA DECIDIR BIEN.”; “FORMA TU CRITERIO”; “CONFIANZA ALTA”;
“CASH · VERIFICADO”; “$142K”.

Art direction: lucid, authoritative, candid, contemporary, and human. Oxide red,
pale lilac, warm bone, and near-black. Custom display typography with decisive
cuts and generous proportions, paired with a very legible sans for product
data. Use proof ticks, annotations, source marks, and confidence intervals as
a living graphic language.

Do not make it look like a bank, law firm, newspaper, university, or generic
consultancy. Avoid editorial minimalism, serif-led luxury, houses, roofs, keys,
doors, skylines, palms, Puerto Rico flag graphics, navy and gold, gradients,
glassmorphism, generic green SaaS, fake words, watermarks, and unreadable filler
text. The result should feel like an original global identity system.

## Marea

Create a vivid, intelligent brand identity concept board for a Spanish-first
real estate investment intelligence platform named MAREA, born in Puerto Rico
and built to expand with Latino investors across the United States. The
platform reveals market movement through verified comparables, proprietary
cash-sale data, ROI analysis, neighborhood trends, and community intelligence.
The strategic idea is to read movement before making a move.

Make one cohesive landscape 16:9 identity presentation with a dominant MAREA
wordmark, a compact M symbol that rises and falls like a market curve, an app
icon, a mobile market screen, a campaign poster, a neighborhood-trend graphic,
a verified cash-deal label, and an expressive data-driven flow field. Use a
bold asymmetric composition, expansive cropping, and a clear hierarchy. Do not
include a typography specimen or name an existing font anywhere in the image.

Exact visible Spanish copy, spelled correctly: “MAREA”; “LEE EL MERCADO.
MUÉVETE MEJOR.”; “VE LO QUE ESTÁ CAMBIANDO.”; “LEE LA MAREA”; “CASH ·
VERIFICADO”; “+8.4%”; “SANTURCE”.

Art direction: alive, confident, warm, and analytical. Cobalt blue, coral,
mineral sand, and deep brown. Wide custom grotesk typography with a rhythmic
baseline, paired with a precise humanist sans for numbers. Build visual motion
from real-looking price points and property data, not decorative waves.

Do not include any font-family name. Do not make a beach, ocean, tourism, surf,
resort, or tropical brand. Avoid literal waves as the main logo, houses, roofs,
keys, doors, skylines, palm trees, Puerto Rico flags, navy and gold, gradients,
glassmorphism, crypto styling, generic green SaaS, fake words, watermarks, and
tiny filler text. It must look like a complete premium identity system with a
strong point of view.

## Cruce

Create a bold, precise brand identity concept board for a Spanish-first real
estate investment intelligence platform named CRUCE, born in Puerto Rico and
built for Latino investors. Its unique advantage is the intersection of CRIM,
Registro, MLS, verified cash-sale reports, and community intelligence. The
strategic idea is that a clearer market picture appears where independent
sources meet.

Make one cohesive landscape 16:9 identity presentation with a dominant CRUCE
wordmark, a simple ownable symbol where independent paths meet at one verified
center, an app icon, a mobile comparable screen, a multi-source provenance
diagram, a campaign poster, a verification badge, and a property report. Use
a forceful asymmetric grid and high-impact scale.

Exact visible Spanish copy, spelled correctly: “CRUCE”; “CRUZA LA DATA.
ENCUENTRA EL DEAL.”; “CUATRO FUENTES. UNA DECISIÓN.”; “CRIM + REGISTRO + MLS +
CASH”; “CRUCE VERIFICADO”; “$127/PC”.

Art direction: connected, forensic, energetic, and accessible. Ultramarine,
tangerine, pale violet, and dark chocolate. Compact custom geometric display
typography with purposeful intersections, paired with a clear product sans.
Use overlays, junctions, transparent source relationships, and highlighted
meeting points.

Do not use road signs, traffic intersections, generic network-node diagrams,
houses, roofs, keys, doors, skylines, palm trees, Puerto Rico flag graphics,
navy and gold, gradients, glassmorphism, crypto styling, generic green SaaS,
fake words, watermarks, or tiny filler text. Make it feel like a strategic
identity case study from a leading independent brand studio.
