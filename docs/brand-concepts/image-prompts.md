## Cabida

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: professional brand identity concept board, 16:9 landscape presentation slide
Primary request: Create a sophisticated, highly polished brand identity concept board for a Spanish-first real estate investment intelligence platform named CABIDA, born in Puerto Rico and built for Latino investors. CABIDA refers to the surface area of a property and also to making room or having a place. The brand promise is credible market context and confident decisions using verified comparables, proprietary cash-sale data, ROI tools, and community intelligence.
Style/medium: elite international brand studio presentation, crisp vector identity system, photographed applications mixed with flat graphic design, distinctive and practical rather than speculative, Pentagram or Koto level of craft without imitating a specific project
Composition/framing: one cohesive landscape board with a dominant CABIDA wordmark, a simple abstract mark built from an open parcel boundary or enclosing brackets with one deliberate opening, palette swatches, typography sample, app icon, one mobile product screen, a verified cash-deal data label, a parcel-map/grid graphic, and one physical application such as a street sign or folded report. Strong asymmetric composition with a rigorous underlying grid and generous breathing room.
Color palette: warm limestone off-white, very dark cacao-black, committed saturated cobalt blue, and a small vivid safety-orange verification accent. No green brand palette.
Typography: custom-feeling wide humanist grotesk, sturdy and grounded, excellent Spanish accents, confident but welcoming, no serif and no monospace as the main voice
Text (verbatim): "CABIDA"; "MÁS CONTEXTO. MEJORES DEALS."; "DATA REAL · DECISIONES CLARAS"; "CASH · VERIFICADO"; "$142,000"; "127/PC"
Constraints: Spell every supplied word exactly. The concept should feel born from land measurement, verified data, and access, not from buying a luxury home. Show a coherent repeatable identity system that can live on a serious analytics product and a warm community brand. Make the logo vector-friendly and simple enough for an app icon.
Avoid: house roof symbols, keys, doors, skylines, palm trees, Puerto Rico flag colors used literally, gold and navy luxury real estate, gradients, glassmorphism, crypto aesthetics, generic green SaaS, tiny unreadable filler text, excessive rounded cards, fake logos, watermarks, and decorative mockup clutter.
```

## Despeja

```text

In Spanish, despejar means to clear away confusion and also to solve for an unknown in an equation. The platform reveals hidden cash-sale comparables, brings scattered Puerto Rico property sources together, runs ROI calculations, organizes deals, and gives investors an AI copilot that acts. It should make expert analysis feel direct and accessible to both first-time and active Latino investors.
Style/medium: elite international challenger-brand presentation, crisp vector identity, graphic and energetic, a memorable identity system with product applications, Koto or Pentagram level of craft without imitating a specific project
Composition/framing: one cohesive landscape board with a dominant DESPEJA wordmark; a simple D-shaped mark where an obscuring block slides away to reveal a precise point or answer; a visual sequence where messy overlapping property records and numbers resolve into one clear result; palette swatches; expressive typography; app icon; a mobile product screen; a bold campaign poster; verified cash-deal badge; and an ROI result module. Strong asymmetric composition, oversized type, deliberate negative space, high impact.
Color palette: electric lemon yellow as the committed hero color, deep ripe-plum almost-black, clear sky blue, and warm paper-white. No green and no gradients.
Typography: expressive contemporary grotesk with open counters and a slight forward energy, paired with a very legible neutral sans for product data; no serif and no monospace as the main voice
Text (verbatim): "DESPEJA"; "DESPEJA LA DUDA. DECIDE CON DATA."; "LO QUE OTROS NO VEN, TÚ LO PUEDES CALCULAR."; "CASH · VERIFICADO"; "¿VALE LA PENA?"; "SÍ · 14.2% ROI"; "X = TU PRÓXIMO DEAL"
Constraints: Spell every supplied word exactly, including accents and punctuation. The mark must be simple and vector-friendly at app-icon size. The visual system should communicate removal of noise, solving for the unknown, and decisive action. It must feel credible for financial decisions but more accessible and energetic than traditional real estate software.
Avoid: house roof symbols, keys, doors, skylines, palm trees, Puerto Rico flag graphics, gold/navy luxury real estate, generic tropical branding, gradients, glassmorphism, crypto aesthetics, generic green SaaS, childish classroom math, generic sparkle AI motifs, excessive rounded cards, fake brand names, watermarks, tiny illegible filler text.
```
